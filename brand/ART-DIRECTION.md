# Art direction

Route: vector (SVG). Flat and matte like the site; the site has no glass or depth, so the mark has none.

## Rejected: the lazy literal
A padlock, a shield, a treasure chest or a gold coin. Each says "crypto vault" and could be any of fifty products. A "T" monogram was the first placeholder (seed d) and is also rejected: a letter in a box says nothing about this product.

## Signature element
A portrait ticket in amber with a half-circle punch at each side of one line and a dashed perforation between the punches. It is the loan ticket from the Borrow screen, and the perforation is the stub line where "what the chain shows" is torn off. Amber on an ink rounded square, centered, wide margin.

## Forbidden
Letters or numbers; padlock, shield, key, coin, chest; hexagon or node graphs; gradients, glow, sparkle; a second hue; bar-chart shapes (seed b drifted there); anything that reads as a monitor or laptop (seeds c, e, f drifted there); the word "anonymous" in any caption.

## Legibility target
Tab at 16px: an amber upright card with the two punch notches still visible. Nav at 32px: perforation dashes visible. 512px: dashes and punches crisp.
