# Brand truth

- name: Trove
- purpose: Borrow dollars against ZEC held in a shielded vault that needs two of three keys to open.
- core_verb: set aside (lock collateral, get a ticket for what is shown).
- metaphor: the loan ticket with a tear-off stub. The site's one signature device prints what a public chain viewer sees on that stub; the mark is that ticket.
- palette: amber #F4B728 on ink #12151A (the site's own accent and ink; ground #F4F5F7). Nothing else.
- constraints: reads at 16px (tab), 32px (nav), 180px (app icon); vector SVG; no letters, no text, no padlock/shield/coin/chest; Brand origin is model-made (design/AUDIENCE.md), owner can replace it.
