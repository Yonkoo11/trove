ZEC collateral goes into a shielded vault, and a public viewer of the lock transaction sees the fee and encrypted fields.

A fixed-term dollar loan against ZEC. The vault key is split three ways: borrower, lender, keeper. Any two can spend, and the loan rules decide which two.

Verify it yourselfRead the limits

Borrower

Holds one key. Repays with the lender, or cancels with the keeper before the loan is paid.

Lender

Holds one key. Takes the repayment with the borrower, or the collateral with the keeper on default.

Keeper

Holds one key. Signs a price reading, and joins the lender on default or the borrower on cancel.

What this does not hide or stop

  • The stablecoin leg is public. Principal and repayment cross chains and are visible there.
  • Policy is software, not chain law. Any two of the three keys can spend. Keeper and lender together can take the collateral. The loan rules in this repo decide who signs each payout; Zcash does not.
  • The price is the keeper's reading. It is signed, not verified on-chain. One live source was checked; two others are tested only on sample replies.
  • The FROST crates are unaudited, the demo tool is marked not for production, and the orchard fork with FROST support is unmerged upstream.
  • Time comes from the driver. The rules trust the event time they are given.
  • Still open: a lender can hold back a newer price and submit an older one that is still fresh, bounded by the price age limit. A lender who pays more than the principal is not refunded the extra. One vault wallet serves every loan on regtest. Late, short and over-locked deposits are now refunded in the rules; that is unit-tested, not re-run on chain.
  • Regtest only. Nothing here ran on a public Zcash network.
