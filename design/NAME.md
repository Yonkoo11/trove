# Name

Job: a one-word name for a private ZEC-backed loan desk. Three keys (borrower, lender, keeper), two of three needed to open the vault. Easy to say, no explaining.

Comparables: Maker, Aave, Compound, Liquity, Morpho (plain or short words, no suffixes).

Owner rejected: Pledge (too common), Wadset/Vadium/Gage/Hypothec (archaic), Keepsake (lame, two-part feel). Wants one word that suits the product.

Finalists (name-check.py run 2026-10-22, web search per finalist):
- Trine: three-fold, matches the three keys. No crypto-listing clash on CoinGecko. Near-neighbours only: dTRINITY and TrueFi Trinity (different word). Trine is also a video game.
- Sealed: vault, closed envelope. No clash found; Ghost Finance and SiennaLend are private lenders under other names.
- Killed: Trove (Liquity calls its debt positions Troves), Quorum (ConsenSys Quorum), Triad (Triadmarkets), Tripod (weak), Plinth (Cardano language), Ballast, Gild, Kiln, Dock, Harbor, Keel, Stash, Veil, Cloak.

Pick: Trine. Runner-up: Sealed.
Unverified: trademark, social handles, domains (all plain words are taken on com/io/app/xyz; pick a variant). Owner decides; NAME in site/app.js stays "Shielded Lend" until then.
